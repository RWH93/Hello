# R&R Detailing website

Customer website for premium mobile car washing and detailing on Brisbane Northside.

## Website files

`dist/index.html` contains the complete website: layout, styling, booking-email form and embedded original logo. No installation or build step is needed. A static website host can serve the `dist` folder directly.

## What customers can do

- Compare exterior ($70), interior ($110) and full detail ($170) packages.
- Choose a package and prepare a booking enquiry in their own email app.
- Call 0405 848 867 or email bookings@rrdetailing.co.

The form does not send email itself, store customer details, reserve appointments or take payments. Customers must send the prepared email. Bookings and final prices are confirmed personally by R&R Detailing.

Prices and service descriptions come from the supplied business flyer. Prices apply to a standard 4-door car in reasonable condition and may vary with size and condition.

## Editing

Edit `dist/index.html` to update prices, wording or contact details. Update both the service cards and the service dropdown when changing packages. The logo is embedded as a JPEG data URL so the page remains self-contained.

The `.openai/hosting.json` file links the working copy to its private Sites preview. It contains no password or secret.
